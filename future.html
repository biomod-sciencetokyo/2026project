2. Improved Future Design

In our current fundamental design, the cholesterol-modified hairpins anchor to both target and non-target (normal) cells. Although they remain inert and do not disrupt normal cells without the trigger, physical anchoring itself is not ideal for in vivo applications.

Next-Generation H1 & H2

We are designing advanced hairpins where the cholesterol molecules are structurally hidden within the DNA. The cholesterols will be exposed ONLY upon the initiation of the HCR trigger, ensuring that the system does not anchor to normal cells at all, drastically improving biosafety.

3. Application to Cancer Cell Killing

Cancer remains a persistent global health crisis. Current molecular-targeted drugs often struggle with endosomal escape, delivery limits, and acquired drug resistance. We envision applying our system as a physical, non-chemical molecular therapy.

By programming the HCR initiator to recognize cancer-specific biomarkers, such as miR-21, the reaction is exclusively triggered upon contact with malignant cells. The resulting rigid DNA bundles pierce and destroy the cancer cell membrane entirely by mechanical force, bypassing traditional chemical resistance pathways.
