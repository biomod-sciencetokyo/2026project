1. Ethical Considerations

Minimizing Off-Target Toxicity

The core ethical advantage of our Stimuli-Responsive Membrane Disruption System lies in its extreme selectivity. Traditional chemotherapy and even advanced targeted nanotherapies often cause collateral damage to healthy tissues. Our system remains entirely inert until it physically encounters a specific disease biomarker (e.g., miR-21). This boolean logic ("AND" gate) ensures that membrane rupture is confined strictly to pathological cells, upholding the ethical mandate of non-maleficence in medical treatments.

2. Biosafety & Security

Material Safety of DNA Nanotechnology

Unlike synthetic polymers or heavy-metal nanoparticles, our structural material is entirely composed of nucleic acids (DNA). DNA is inherently biocompatible and biodegradable. In the event of systemic administration, disassembled or degraded DNA strands can be safely metabolized by endogenous nucleases (e.g., DNase I), posing minimal long-term accumulation risks in the environment or the human body.

Laboratory Safety

All wet experiments planned for our proof of concept will be conducted using synthetic lipid vesicles (liposomes) and commercially synthesized DNA oligos. No pathogenic organisms, live viruses, or infectious human samples are required for the fundamental validation, adhering strictly to Biosafety Level 1 (BSL-1) protocols.
