Open Sesame! Hybridization Chain Reaction-Driven DNA Bundling
for Lipid Membrane Disruption

Targeted EV membrane disruption using rigid DNA bundles assembled via in situ HCR.

SCROLL

Abstract

Extracellular vesicles (EVs) carry molecular cargo that is valuable for diagnosis, yet conventional disruption methods destroy origin information.

This project uses a target-triggered Hybridization Chain Reaction (HCR) to assemble rigid DNA bundles on lipid membranes in situ, mechanically disrupting only the intended vesicles.

Vision

Future Vision Image Placeholder

Potentially, our research may pave the way for molecular signal-responsive systems capable of lipid membrane disruption.

By using cell-free cancer-associated miRNAs as sequence-specific triggers, such systems could eventually be extended from lipid vesicles toward larger membrane-bound targets, including living cells.
