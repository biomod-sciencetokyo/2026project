1. System Overview

Our system relies on two types of specially designed hairpin DNA (H1 and H2). Each hairpin is modified with a cholesterol anchor via a TEG linker, allowing them to spontaneously bind to the lipid bilayer of the target membrane. They remain inert until activated by a specific biomarker.
