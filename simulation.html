Membranedisruption

2DMD simulation

To understand the fundamental physical mechanisms by which DNA bundles deform and rupture extracellular vesicle (EV) membranes, we established a two-dimensional Coarse-Grained Molecular Dynamics (CG-MD) simulation. A 2D cross-sectional model allows us to clearly isolate and observe the fundamental competition between the bending stiffness of the DNA bundle and the elasticity of the lipid membrane.

In this environment, the experimental system is coarse-grained—meaning groups of atoms are represented as single "beads"—comprising the DNA bundle, cholesterol anchors, flexible linkers, and the EV membrane lipid bilayer. This simplification enables us to effectively simulate large-scale structural dynamics over extended timescales.
